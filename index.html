Seamless setup.

  • Copy the repository link from the top of this page.
  • Open AltStore or SideStore on your iOS device.
  • Navigate to the 'Sources' tab and tap the '+' button.
  • Paste the URL and tap 'Add'. The IPAs will load instantly.
  • Copy the repository link provided above.
  • Open ESign, Scarlet, or K-Sign.
  • Go to App Store / Source settings inside the app.
  • Add a new source, paste the JSON URL, and save. Refresh the page to see apps.
  • Copy the source JSON link.
  • Launch Feather from your home screen.
  • Tap on Settings -> Repositories.
  • Add the copied URL. The app library will sync automatically.
